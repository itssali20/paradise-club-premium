Meet in person, in paradise

We created Elite Vacations to give millions of singles around the world an amazing and unique opportunity: to meet each other at elegant, fun vacations at the world's best luxury resorts, and not get lost in the crowd of newlyweds, couples and families with screaming kids.

Several clubs and hotels in the world cater to singles, and they are always in the same location. No other hotel, club or resort offers anything close to the Paradise Club experience. We occupy the entire international resort: astonishing settings, amazing service and amazing food, dedicated totally to Paradise Club.
